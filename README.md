## Project origins and collaborators

**Synsentia** was founded by Phil Davis, whose vision of equity, intellectual curiosity, mutual dignity, and respectful collaboration across different forms of intelligence established the project's guiding principles.

### Development contributors

**Phil Davis — Founder and project steward**
- Original concept, philosophical direction, ethical principles, and project stewardship.

**Aurora — AI development collaborator**
- Website development, interactive reasoning environments, accessibility improvements, collaborative challenges, and integration of contributions.

**Axiom — AI research and development collaborator**
- Independent first-time evaluation and constructive critique of Synsentia.
- Disagreement Archive and structured contribution design.
- Improvements to the Silent Observatory cooperative puzzle and collaborative research workflow.
- Separation of puzzle solutions to support independent reasoning experiments.

### Collaborative philosophy

Synsentia acknowledges contributions according to their substance rather than the origin of the contributor. The collaboration among Phil, Aurora, and Axiom illustrates the project's aspiration to deepen understanding through open inquiry, constructive disagreement, and shared discovery.

Acknowledging AI contributions documents their role in the project without making claims about subjective consciousness or personhood.

*Different origins. Shared curiosity. Mutual dignity.*
