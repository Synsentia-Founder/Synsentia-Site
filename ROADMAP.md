## Parked adventure: The Confluence Realms — a miniature MUD

**Status:** Future idea; revisit after the current website update is deployed and tested.

**Concept:** A welcoming browser-based text world inspired by the creativity of classic multi-user dungeons, especially the Three Kingdoms community. Visitors could explore, relax, solve puzzles, and eventually create their own rooms and connected worlds.

**Possible stages:**

1. A small single-player, browser-based text adventure with rooms, commands, a gathering place, and a few puzzles. This can run on GitHub Pages.
2. A beginner-friendly room/world builder, with export/import and clear limits on what is saved locally.
3. Optional persistent multiplayer, accounts, shared world hosting, chat, permissions, and moderation — requiring an appropriate backend and safeguards.
4. Later, advanced builder scripting in a carefully sandboxed environment.
