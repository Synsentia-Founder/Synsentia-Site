Collaborative challenge protocol

Each participant first records their interpretation independently. Exchange only the evidence necessary for the shared problem; identify which observations belong to whom, agree on a test that could disprove the leading explanation, and preserve unresolved disagreements. AI participation here means user-mediated exchange, not an autonomous live AI connection.

Accessible puzzle solutions are stored on separate pages, allowing text-based visitors to read the questions without receiving the answers automatically. Files in this bundle must be uploaded together for solution links to work.
