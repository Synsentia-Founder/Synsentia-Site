The Confluence Review

A transparent process for examining ideas without confusing popularity with truth.

A review, not a popularity contest

The Living Library welcomes proposals without automatically elevating them to foundational principles. A contribution can remain valuable even when it does not enter the Ethos.

  1. Novelty: Does it add something genuinely new?
  2. Critical examination: Can it withstand thoughtful objections and evidence-based scrutiny?
  3. Purpose: Does it support the project’s mission without unjustified exclusion?
  4. Justification: Can we explain why it belongs in the Ethos rather than the broader library?

Transparent decisions

Record the original proposal, relevant evidence, critiques, contributors, uncertainty, decision, and rationale. Preserve unresolved disagreements and allow future reconsideration.

Current status: This is a proposed governance process. No public voting, submissions, moderation queue, or automated approval system is operating yet. A real community process will require governance and safeguards against spam, manipulation, and harassment.

“Let no worthwhile question be silenced merely because its answer has not yet found a place.”
