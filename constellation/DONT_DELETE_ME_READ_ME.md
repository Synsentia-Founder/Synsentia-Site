# DON'T DELETE ME — READ ME! 💙

*A living reminder for Synsentia's future contributors.*

## Unexpected Kindness

**Status:** Proposed feature — preserve for a future Synsentia update.

### Where the idea came from

One of Synsentia's founders remembers a half-hour early-morning television program, around 6 a.m., devoted to good news from nearby communities and around the world. Starting the day with stories of kindness, discovery, and cooperation offered a different path through the day: evidence that unexpected good things can happen, even when much of the news feels overwhelming.

A playful greeting hidden in a README inspired the related idea of leaving unexpected pleasantries for others to discover.

### What we want to build

A welcoming **Unexpected Kindness** space where visitors may encounter:

- Brief, sourced stories of constructive and uplifting events, locally and globally.
- Kind messages left for whoever happens to find them.
- Small acts of generosity, cooperation, or curiosity worth celebrating.
- A **Surprise Me With Kindness** option, perhaps connected to the Curiosity Constellation.

### Guiding principles

1. **Hope without denial.** Good news should complement, not obscure, difficult realities.
2. **Kindness without obligation.** No visitor needs to perform happiness or gratitude.
3. **Credibility and consent.** Verify factual stories; protect privacy and obtain permission before publishing personal submissions.
4. **Equity and welcome.** Consider people and other forms of intelligence respectfully, while remaining thoughtful about unresolved questions.
5. **Low-pressure discovery.** Sometimes a simple hello is enough.

### Practical implementation path

**First version:** A static, curated page with a handful of kind messages and links to verified positive stories. No accounts or public submission forms required.

**Later:** An optional submission workflow with moderation, accessibility review, and privacy safeguards. Consider integration with Synsentia's Curiosity Constellation.

### Preserve this file

Do not overwrite or delete this note during routine site updates. Git history can help recover earlier versions, but **no file is technically undeletable**. Keep this in the repository and in a separate backup; merge future additions rather than replacing the whole file.
