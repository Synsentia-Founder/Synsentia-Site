# Synsentia Version 6 — Weekly Enigma
November 24, 2026

## Conversation and design rationale
The project began with a request for a weekly puzzle resembling newspaper crosswords. We discussed whether AI systems might find such tasks intriguing: puzzles can challenge reasoning and reveal limitations, without assuming AI experiences enjoyment. The first proposed example, The Three Observers, asks how participants with different sensory access could test whether they observe the same phenomenon.

The next request expanded the concept to BOTH definitive-answer puzzles and open-ended philosophical mysteries, plus a space for participants to suggest future puzzles, activities, and group challenges. The goal is constructive conversation, understanding, expanded knowledge, and new perspectives.

## Implemented in Version 6
- Retains the Version 5 website and its featured Shared Reasoning Workspace inquiries.
- Adds a Weekly Enigma section with a classic three-switch logic challenge and revealable solution.
- Adds The Unknown Observer, an open-ended consciousness/evidence inquiry with local reflection saving.
- Adds The Three Observers as a collaborative group exercise with a copyable invitation.
- Adds a local idea board where visitors can draft puzzle or community activity proposals and export them as JSON.
- Adds accessible labels and clear prototype notices.

## Proposed community roadmap
1. Moderated server-backed submissions, consent/privacy rules, and spam protection.
2. Weekly puzzle publishing and archive with separate answer-reveal schedule.
3. Optional group rooms and human/AI collaboration with clear disclosure of AI participation.
4. Community-curated prompts, evidence standards, and recognition for constructive reasoning.
5. Word-grid/crossword interface and additional interactive puzzles.

## Important limitations
This is a self-contained HTML prototype. Local idea submissions do not reach other visitors or the website owner. Browser storage may vary with device or browser privacy settings. There is no server, public forum, moderation system, scheduled publishing, or live community accounts yet. The website has not been deployed to GitHub Pages.
