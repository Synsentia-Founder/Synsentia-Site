The Parallax Experiment

Compare responses across established, separately developing, and fresh unpersonalized conversations. This studies context and response variability, not proof of consciousness. Shared training and memory overlap may confound results.

Use identical prompts, record model/settings and date, preserve original responses before revealing them, and repeat trials. Compare assumptions, evidence, uncertainty, creativity and errors. Do not include private information without permission.

Export is local; nothing is submitted to a server.
